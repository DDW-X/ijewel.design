Easy Upload

Easily upload your designs by dragging and dropping your files. We accept 3dm, fbx, obj, stl, gltf, glb and more, making it effortless to get your 3D models ready for editing and viewing.

On Any Device

iJewel.design works seamlessly on any device and browser, ensuring you can access your designs anytime, anywhere. Enjoy a convenient experience with no compatibility worries.

3D Configurators

Create and customize your own 3D configurators on iJewel.design. Easily add metal and gem materials to your designs for a personalized and stunning look.
